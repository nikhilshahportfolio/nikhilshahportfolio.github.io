Ministry of Misconduct

(Work in progress)
p5js game



a dystopian document thriller

You are appointed as a government minister – after surveilling the city, you have been found to be a somewhat corrupt person.

You must choose which documents to work on and which to destroy. To be in the job, you have to maintain the aura of corruption. You can either stamp a document or destroy it in pieces.
